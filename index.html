📋 Your Session

Breathing Foundation

  • Posture: Feet flat, shoulders relaxed, chin level. Stand or sit tall — not stiff.
  • Diaphragm Breath: Hand on belly → breathe in through nose → belly expands, shoulders still. Out slowly through pursed lips like blowing out a candle.
  • Pattern: In 4 counts → Hold 2 → Out 6–8 counts. Calm, steady, silent breath in.
  • Warm-up: Deep breath in → steady "Aaaaaah" as long and smooth as you can.

Pausing — Your Secret Weapon

⏸️ Short Pause — 1 beat

Between phrases / ideas. Silence = comma in writing.

Example: "I want to speak clearly ⏸ and confidently."

⏹️ Long Pause — 2–3 beats

Between sentences / before key words. Silence = period or new thought.

Example: "Practice every day ⏹ You WILL improve."

💡 Rule: Pause BEFORE the important word — not after. Let the silence build attention.

Pronunciation Guide

  • Stress = louder & longer: TIK in ar-TIK-yuh-layt. Only 1 main stress per word.
  • TH sounds: Tongue tip lightly touching top teeth. Think — tongue between teeth, not behind them!
  • R vs L: R = lips round, tongue pulled back. L = tongue tip touches roof of mouth behind front teeth.
  • Endings matter: Don't drop final sounds — make every letter count without rushing.
